# Phase 01 procurement worksheet

> **Research only — do not order until the ISP handoff and model-level compatibility are verified.**

| Category | Minimum requirement | Qty | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|
| OPNsense firewall | 10GbE WAN and LAN paths, supported NICs, throughput sized for enabled inspection | 1 | Research |
| Managed switch | 802.1Q VLANs, appropriate 10GbE ports, sufficient 2.5GbE access ports | 1 | Research |
| Patch cabling | Cat6a or qualified Cat6 for anticipated lengths and speeds | As needed | Research |
| UPS | Rated for measured load, shutdown signaling, runtime | 1 | Research |
| Wi-Fi AP | Reuse existing AP-capable router initially | 0 new | Validate |
| Tailscale node | Existing compatible system or VM initially | 0 new | Planned |

## Buying gate

- [ ] Confirm ISP 5 Gbps handoff speed, port type, authentication, and gateway constraints
- [ ] Verify WAN and LAN interface compatibility end to end
- [ ] Validate firewall throughput with security features enabled
- [ ] Confirm switch 802.1Q, SFP+ / RJ45 requirements and optics
- [ ] Check physical space, thermals, acoustics and power
- [ ] Record verified model numbers and prices before placing orders
